Rahu & Ketu: Understanding Your Karmic Nodes

7 min read

Rahu and Ketu aren't planets in the way Mars or Jupiter are, they're mathematical points, the two places where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's apparent path, the exact locations where eclipses happen. In Vedic astrology, they are treated as full players in the chart, and they carry some of the most important information about your soul's direction: your Rahu, and its opposite point, Ketu.

Where most planets describe personality or circumstance, the nodes describe momentum. Rahu is where you're being pulled forward, often into unfamiliar, slightly uncomfortable territory. Ketu is what you're meant to release, the mastery, and the attachment, you're already carrying from somewhere deep, possibly from before this life.

Ketu: What You're Meant to Let Go Of

Ketu's house and sign show where you already have an old, instinctive fluency, and where you tend to disengage too easily, precisely because it comes so naturally that it no longer feels like growth. This is often the area of life people describe as "I'm just not that interested," when what's actually happening is a soul-level over-familiarity.

Rahu: Where You're Being Pulled

Rahu's placement points toward what will feel unnatural, even slightly obsessive, and deeply uncomfortable at first, and also toward where your greatest growth this lifetime is waiting. Rahu themes often show up as a persistent craving: for recognition, for a certain kind of relationship, for a version of stability you didn't grow up with. That craving isn't a flaw to fix. It's a direction to walk toward, carefully and consciously.

Ketu is the mastery you're meant to stop hiding behind. Rahu is the discomfort you're meant to walk into anyway.

Working With the Axis

The nodes always sit exactly opposite each other in the chart, which is the whole point: you're not meant to abandon Ketu's gifts entirely, or chase Rahu blindly. The real work of a life is learning to bring Ketu's quiet mastery in service of Rahu's growth, using what you already know how to do in pursuit of what still scares you a little.

This is one of the richest, and most specific, parts of a soul path reading, mapping exactly where your nodes fall and what they're asking of you in this chapter of your life.
