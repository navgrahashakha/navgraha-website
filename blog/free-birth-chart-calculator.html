I Built a Free Vedic Birth Chart Calculator — Here's How to Use It

4 min read

People ask me their Sun, Moon, and Rising sign constantly, usually before they even know if they want a full reading. So there's now a free Vedic Birth Chart Calculator on the site: enter your birth date, time, and place, and get your Ascendant, all nine planetary placements, and your Moon's Nakshatra, instantly, in your browser.

What It Actually Calculates

This isn't a Western tropical chart wearing Vedic labels, it uses the Lahiri ayanamsa, the sidereal system Indian Vedic astrologers actually work from, and Whole Sign houses, the classical approach from BPHS. You'll see your Ascendant (Lagna), where the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu fall by sign and house, and a simple South Indian style chart you can read at a glance.

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. The whole calculation runs in your own browser, and closing the page clears it.

A calculator can show you the placements. A reading tells you what they mean.

Why Your Birth Time Matters So Much Here

Your Ascendant shifts roughly every two hours, and every house placement in your chart is calculated relative to it. If your birth time is off by even fifteen or twenty minutes, near a sign boundary that can shift your entire house structure. If you're not certain of your exact time, check your birth certificate or hospital records before relying on the result.

What to Do With the Results

Treat this as your starting map, not the whole territory. Knowing your Moon is in a certain sign or Nakshatra is useful, but what it actually means for your patterns, your relationships, your timing, is where a real reading goes further than any calculator can. If something in your results makes you curious, that curiosity is usually worth following into an actual conversation.
