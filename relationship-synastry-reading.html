What This Reading Covers

Compatibility, Beyond Just Romance

Synastry is the comparison of two birth charts to understand how two people move together, where you naturally align, where friction tends to show up, and what the relationship is actually built to teach you both. It applies to any close relationship: a partner, a close friend, a family member, a business partner.

This reading looks at both individual chart placements against each other (synastry) and the relationship’s own combined chart (composite), giving a fuller picture than either alone.
