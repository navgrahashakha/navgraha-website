Pentacles Tarot Card Meanings

The suit of work, money, and the material world.

Ace of Pentacles Two of Pentacles Three of Pentacles Four of Pentacles Five of Pentacles Six of Pentacles Seven of Pentacles Eight of Pentacles Nine of Pentacles Ten of Pentacles Page of Pentacles Knight of Pentacles Queen of Pentacles King of Pentacles

Ace of Pentacles

Upright: new opportunity, financial beginning, manifestation, security

Reversed: missed opportunity, poor financial planning, lack of foresight

A tangible new opportunity, worth taking seriously and following through on.

Two of Pentacles

Upright: balance, adaptability, juggling priorities, time management

Reversed: overwhelm, disorganization, dropping the ball

Keeping multiple things moving at once, manageable with the right rhythm, harder without one.

Three of Pentacles

Upright: teamwork, collaboration, skill, building something together

Reversed: lack of teamwork, disorganization, working alone when help is needed

Something built well because more than one set of hands and skills went into it.

Four of Pentacles

Upright: security, saving, control, holding on

Reversed: greed, letting go, financial insecurity

Holding tightly to what you have, sometimes wise caution, sometimes fear dressed up as security.

Five of Pentacles

Upright: financial hardship, insecurity, feeling left out in the cold

Reversed: recovery, asking for help, improving circumstances

A genuinely hard stretch, materially or otherwise, worth remembering that help is often closer than it feels.

Six of Pentacles

Upright: generosity, giving and receiving, charity, balance of power

Reversed: strings attached, one-sided generosity, debt

An exchange, giving or receiving, worth noticing which side of it you’re actually on.

Seven of Pentacles

Upright: patience, long-term investment, assessment, waiting on growth

Reversed: impatience, lack of long-term vision, wasted effort

A pause to assess whether the effort you’ve put in is actually paying off, before deciding what’s next.

Eight of Pentacles

Upright: craftsmanship, dedication, skill-building, hard work

Reversed: perfectionism, lack of focus, cutting corners

Putting in the repetitions that actually build mastery, unglamorous, but effective.

Nine of Pentacles

Upright: self-sufficiency, earned abundance, independence

Reversed: financial setback, overworking, dependency

Enjoying something you built and earned yourself, comfort that came from your own effort.

Ten of Pentacles

Upright: legacy, long-term security, family wealth, lasting foundations

Reversed: financial loss, family disputes, broken legacy

Security that outlasts any one moment, something built to be passed on.

Page of Pentacles

Upright: new opportunity, studiousness, manifestation, a practical start

Reversed: lack of follow-through, unrealistic goals, procrastination

A practical new opportunity or plan, worth taking seriously even if it feels small right now.

Knight of Pentacles

Upright: hard work, reliability, routine, patience

Reversed: laziness, obsessiveness, stagnation

Steady, unglamorous effort, the kind that actually finishes what it starts.

Queen of Pentacles

Upright: nurturing, practical, resourceful, grounded abundance

Reversed: financial insecurity, neglecting self-care, smothering

Care that shows up in tangible ways, a home, a meal, a budget that actually works.

King of Pentacles

Upright: financial security, discipline, abundance, stable leadership

Reversed: greed, materialism, stubbornness

Security built over time through discipline, generous because there’s actually something to give from.
