Privacy Policy

How Navgraha Shakha collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

LAST UPDATED: JANUARY 2025

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Rushali Thukral under the brand Navgraha Shakha, an astrology, tarot, and spiritual coaching practice based in Goa, India. You can contact us at navgrahashakha@gmail.com.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Fill out the contact or booking form on this website
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Communicate with us via email or WhatsApp

The information we collect may include your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, birth time and place (for astrology readings), and any details you share in your message.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and confirm your booking
  • To deliver astrology, tarot, and spiritual coaching services you have booked
  • To prepare your birth chart or reading materials
  • To send you newsletters, updates, new service announcements, blog posts, promotions, and other marketing communications from Navgraha Shakha
  • To improve our services and website

4. Marketing Communications

By submitting your details through any form on this website, you consent to receiving marketing communications from Navgraha Shakha. You can opt out at any time by emailing us at navgrahashakha@gmail.com with the subject line "Unsubscribe".

5. Data Storage and Security

Your personal information is stored securely and is accessible only to Rushali Thukral. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

6. Birth Data and Sensitive Information

Birth date, birth time, and birth place shared for astrology readings are used solely for the purpose of preparing your reading. This information is treated with the highest level of confidentiality and is never shared with third parties.

7. Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Request that we stop using your data for any purpose

To exercise any of these rights, please email navgrahashakha@gmail.com. We will respond within 14 business days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at navgrahashakha@gmail.com.
