Cups Tarot Card Meanings

The suit of emotion, relationships, and the inner world.

Ace of Cups Two of Cups Three of Cups Four of Cups Five of Cups Six of Cups Seven of Cups Eight of Cups Nine of Cups Ten of Cups Page of Cups Knight of Cups Queen of Cups King of Cups

Ace of Cups

Upright: new love, emotional beginnings, compassion, overflowing feeling

Reversed: emotional loss, blocked feelings, emptiness

The start of something felt rather than thought, an open heart ready to receive.

Two of Cups

Upright: partnership, mutual attraction, connection, union

Reversed: imbalance in a relationship, broken communication, disconnection

A genuine meeting between two people, feeling seen and seeing someone back.

Three of Cups

Upright: friendship, celebration, community, shared joy

Reversed: overindulgence, gossip, isolation from your circle

Joy that’s better because it’s shared, friendships and community worth celebrating.

Four of Cups

Upright: apathy, contemplation, missed opportunity, discontent

Reversed: renewed interest, waking up to what’s being offered

Sitting with what you have but not quite seeing it, an offer close by you haven’t noticed yet.

Five of Cups

Upright: loss, regret, grief, focusing on what’s gone

Reversed: acceptance, moving on, finding peace

Grief that’s real, but often paired with two cups still standing you haven’t turned to see yet.

Six of Cups

Upright: nostalgia, childhood memories, innocence, reunion

Reversed: living in the past, stuck in old patterns, unrealistic nostalgia

Warmth from the past showing up in the present, sometimes a person, sometimes just a feeling.

Seven of Cups

Upright: choices, illusion, wishful thinking, too many options

Reversed: clarity, cutting through illusion, making a real decision

A lot of appealing options, not all of them real, discernment matters more here than desire.

Eight of Cups

Upright: walking away, seeking something deeper, disillusionment

Reversed: fear of moving on, staying too long, avoidance

Leaving something behind, not out of anger, but because it stopped being enough.

Nine of Cups

Upright: satisfaction, contentment, wishes fulfilled, emotional wellbeing

Reversed: overindulgence, smugness, hollow satisfaction

The "wish card," a genuine sense of having what you wanted, or being close to it.

Ten of Cups

Upright: emotional fulfillment, harmony, family happiness, lasting joy

Reversed: disconnection within family, broken harmony, unrealistic expectations

The long-view happiness, not a single moment but a life that feels emotionally whole.

Page of Cups

Upright: emotional openness, curiosity, creative beginnings, a message

Reversed: emotional immaturity, insecurity, unrealistic ideas

Approaching feelings with openness and imagination rather than caution.

Knight of Cups

Upright: romance, following the heart, an offer, idealism

Reversed: moodiness, unrealistic expectations, disappointment

Acting from feeling, a romantic gesture or offer made with real sincerity.

Queen of Cups

Upright: compassion, emotional security, intuition, nurturing

Reversed: emotional insecurity, moodiness, martyrdom

Emotional maturity that can hold space for others without losing herself in it.

King of Cups

Upright: emotional balance, diplomacy, calm control of feeling

Reversed: emotional manipulation, moodiness, volatility

Someone who feels deeply but doesn’t let feeling run the room, calm strength built on emotional maturity.
