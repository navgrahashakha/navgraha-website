What This Reading Covers

Rahu, Ketu & Your Deeper Direction

Rahu and Ketu, the lunar nodes, are the two points in your chart that map the pull between where you’re meant to grow this lifetime and what you’re meant to release. Rahu points toward unfamiliar, sometimes uncomfortable growth. Ketu points toward an old, instinctive mastery you may be over-relying on.

A Soul Path Reading works with this axis specifically, alongside the rest of your chart, to explore your life’s purpose and dharma, not fortune-telling, but a way of understanding the direction your soul already seems to be moving in.
