What This Reading Covers

More Than a Compatibility Score

In Vedic astrology, marriage compatibility isn't decided by one number. Ashtakoot matching, the classical system comparing two charts across eight factors, gives you a Guna score out of 36, but the score alone doesn't tell you why two people click or where the friction will actually show up.

This reading goes past the score to look at the karmic bonds between your two charts, the themes you're likely to build a life around together, and the timing questions couples actually ask, when to marry, what to prepare for, and what the union's long-term potential looks like.
