What This Reading Covers

A Mirror, Not a Fortune-Telling Machine

Tarot isn’t about predicting your future with certainty, it’s a structured symbolic system, 78 cards built from centuries of archetypes, used to surface what you already sense but haven’t quite named yet. I read within the Rider-Waite tradition, intuitively rather than from a fixed script.

Sessions are built around whatever you’re actually asking: a specific question about career or a relationship, or a more open reading when you’re not sure exactly what you need clarity on. Either way, you’ll leave with a clearer picture, not just a set of predictions.
