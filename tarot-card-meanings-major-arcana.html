Major Arcana Tarot Card Meanings

The 22 cards mapping life's big turning points, from The Fool to The World.

0 · The Fool I · The Magician II · The High Priestess III · The Empress IV · The Emperor V · The Hierophant VI · The Lovers VII · The Chariot VIII · Strength IX · The Hermit X · Wheel of Fortune XI · Justice XII · The Hanged Man XIII · Death XIV · Temperance XV · The Devil XVI · The Tower XVII · The Star XVIII · The Moon XIX · The Sun XX · Judgement XXI · The World

0 · The Fool

Upright: new beginnings, spontaneity, faith in the unknown

Reversed: recklessness, hesitancy, fear of the unknown

Standing at the edge of something new, carrying nothing but trust that the ground will meet you. Not carelessness, but a willingness to begin before you have every answer.

I · The Magician

Upright: manifestation, resourcefulness, skill, taking action

Reversed: manipulation, untapped potential, poor planning

Everything you need is already on the table. This card is the will to combine it and actually act, rather than waiting for more.

II · The High Priestess

Upright: intuition, the unconscious, hidden knowledge, stillness

Reversed: secrets withheld, disconnection from intuition, surface-level thinking

What you know without being able to explain how. Guidance found by going quiet and listening inward, rather than searching outward.

III · The Empress

Upright: abundance, nurturing, creativity, connection to nature

Reversed: creative block, neglect, smothering

Fertile, generous energy, the willingness to create and nurture something, whether that’s a project, a relationship, or yourself.

IV · The Emperor

Upright: structure, authority, stability, discipline

Reversed: rigidity, control issues, absence of structure

The scaffolding that lets freedom actually function. Boundaries, order, and follow-through, not as restriction, but as what makes growth possible.

V · The Hierophant

Upright: tradition, institutions, conventional wisdom, mentorship

Reversed: rebellion against convention, questioning tradition, unconventional paths

Learning within an established structure or lineage, and the choice of when to honour that structure, and when it’s time to step outside it.

VI · The Lovers

Upright: connection, alignment, meaningful choice, shared values

Reversed: misalignment, disharmony, avoidance of a choice

Rarely just about romance. More often a real choice in front of you that asks you to act from your actual values, not what’s easiest.

VII · The Chariot

Upright: willpower, determination, forward motion through opposing forces

Reversed: lack of direction, loss of control, aggression

Holding two opposing forces steady enough to actually move forward. Discipline and focus, not brute force.

VIII · Strength

Upright: courage, patience, inner strength, compassion

Reversed: self-doubt, force over patience, insecurity

The quiet kind of strength. Gentleness that holds its ground, rather than force that tries to overpower.

IX · The Hermit

Upright: introspection, solitude, inner guidance, withdrawal to seek answers

Reversed: isolation, avoidance, loneliness

Stepping back from noise to hear what you actually think. A deliberate pause, not a retreat from life.

X · Wheel of Fortune

Upright: cycles, turning points, change beyond your control, fate

Reversed: resistance to change, bad timing, feeling stuck

A reminder that nothing, good or hard, stays fixed forever. Timing is part of the picture, not something you fully control.

XI · Justice

Upright: fairness, truth, cause and effect, accountability

Reversed: unfairness, avoiding accountability, dishonesty

What you put out eventually returns. A card of clear-eyed honesty and consequence, rather than punishment.

XII · The Hanged Man

Upright: surrender, new perspective, letting go of control

Reversed: stalling, resistance, martyrdom

Pausing, even uncomfortably, until a situation looks different from a new angle. Not defeat, but a deliberate suspension.

XIII · Death

Upright: endings, transformation, release, transition

Reversed: resistance to change, stagnation, fear of letting go

Almost never literal. This is the card of one chapter closing so another can genuinely begin, transformation rather than loss.

XIV · Temperance

Upright: balance, moderation, integration, patience

Reversed: excess, imbalance, impatience

Blending opposing parts of yourself or a situation into something workable. Slow and steady, rather than sudden.

XV · The Devil

Upright: attachment, restriction, unhealthy patterns, material focus

Reversed: breaking free, recognizing a pattern, reclaiming power

The chains in this card are almost always ones you can remove yourself. It’s about naming what’s actually holding you.

XVI · The Tower

Upright: sudden upheaval, revelation, the collapse of a false foundation

Reversed: avoiding disaster, delayed collapse, fear of change

Unsettling, but often necessary. Clearing away something that was never built to last, so something truer can be rebuilt.

XVII · The Star

Upright: hope, renewal, healing, inspiration after hardship

Reversed: despair, disconnection, lost faith

Quiet hope returning after a difficult stretch. Not naive optimism, but trust rebuilding itself, slowly.

XVIII · The Moon

Upright: uncertainty, illusion, intuition, the subconscious

Reversed: releasing fear, clarity emerging, confusion lifting

Moving through a stretch where things aren’t fully clear yet, and trusting instinct even without full visibility.

XIX · The Sun

Upright: joy, vitality, success, clarity

Reversed: temporary setbacks, low energy, delayed joy

About as straightforward as the deck gets. Warmth, clarity, and things genuinely going well.

XX · Judgement

Upright: reflection, reckoning, awakening, a call to a higher purpose

Reversed: self-doubt, avoiding self-reflection, harsh self-judgment

An honest reckoning with where you’ve been, in service of stepping into who you’re becoming.

XXI · The World

Upright: completion, fulfillment, wholeness, integration

Reversed: incompletion, delay, unfinished business

A cycle genuinely closing, not with a bang but with a real sense of arrival, and the doorway to whatever begins next.
