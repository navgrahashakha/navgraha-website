What This Reading Covers

Your Chart, Mapped in Full

A birth chart, or kundli, is a map of exactly where every planet sat in the sky at the moment you were born. In Vedic astrology, this includes all nine Navgrahas, not just the sun and moon, each placed into one of twelve houses that govern a different area of your life: career, relationships, health, family, and more.

This reading walks through your full chart in plain language: what your placements actually mean for how you move through the world, where your natural strengths sit, which karmic patterns keep resurfacing, and what the current planetary transits are asking of you right now.
