Swords Tarot Card Meanings

The suit of thought, conflict, and truth.

Ace of Swords Two of Swords Three of Swords Four of Swords Five of Swords Six of Swords Seven of Swords Eight of Swords Nine of Swords Ten of Swords Page of Swords Knight of Swords Queen of Swords King of Swords

Ace of Swords

Upright: clarity, breakthrough, new idea, truth

Reversed: confusion, misinformation, poor judgment

A sudden moment of mental clarity, cutting straight through confusion to what’s actually true.

Two of Swords

Upright: difficult decision, stalemate, avoidance, weighing options

Reversed: indecision, information overload, releasing a stalemate

A choice being avoided, often because both options feel equally hard, or equally unclear.

Three of Swords

Upright: heartbreak, emotional pain, grief, betrayal

Reversed: healing, forgiveness, moving past pain

Real pain, usually from truth landing somewhere it hurts, not gentle, but often necessary.

Four of Swords

Upright: rest, recovery, contemplation, a pause

Reversed: burnout, restlessness, forced rest

A deliberate pause to recover, mentally or physically, before picking back up.

Five of Swords

Upright: conflict, win at any cost, tension, betrayal

Reversed: reconciliation, moving past conflict, lingering resentment

A win that costs more than it’s worth, worth asking whether being right is actually the goal here.

Six of Swords

Upright: transition, moving on, leaving hardship behind, gradual healing

Reversed: resistance to change, unresolved issues, stuck in transition

Moving away from a difficult stretch, not instantly, but steadily, toward calmer water.

Seven of Swords

Upright: deception, strategy, acting alone, getting away with something

Reversed: coming clean, facing consequences, self-deceit

Cutting corners or working around others, sometimes strategic, sometimes something to be honest with yourself about.

Eight of Swords

Upright: feeling trapped, restriction, self-imposed limitation

Reversed: releasing limiting beliefs, finding freedom, self-empowerment

Feeling stuck in a situation that, on closer look, may have more give than it appears. Often the restriction is the story you’re telling yourself.

Nine of Swords

Upright: anxiety, worry, fear, sleepless nights

Reversed: releasing anxiety, facing fears, finding relief

The 3am spiral, fear that’s often bigger in the mind than in reality, worth naming out loud.

Ten of Swords

Upright: painful ending, betrayal, rock bottom, a difficult conclusion

Reversed: recovery, resisting an ending that’s already happened, gradual healing

A hard ending, but a complete one, nothing left to salvage, and nowhere to go from here but up.

Page of Swords

Upright: curiosity, new ideas, vigilance, mental energy

Reversed: gossip, scattered thinking, all talk no follow-through

A sharp, curious mind, eager to learn and question, not always with the tact to match.

Knight of Swords

Upright: fast action, assertiveness, direct communication, ambition

Reversed: recklessness, impulsiveness, aggression

Moving and speaking quickly, sometimes brilliant, sometimes too fast to consider who’s in the way.

Queen of Swords

Upright: clear thinking, honesty, independence, direct communication

Reversed: coldness, bitterness, using words to hurt

Clarity that doesn’t soften the truth to spare feelings, honesty as a form of respect.

King of Swords

Upright: intellectual authority, truth, fairness, clear judgment

Reversed: manipulation, cruelty, abuse of power

Judgment made from logic rather than emotion, authority earned through clarity, not force.
