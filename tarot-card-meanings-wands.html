Wands Tarot Card Meanings

The suit of ambition, creativity, and action.

Ace of Wands Two of Wands Three of Wands Four of Wands Five of Wands Six of Wands Seven of Wands Eight of Wands Nine of Wands Ten of Wands Page of Wands Knight of Wands Queen of Wands King of Wands

Ace of Wands

Upright: inspiration, new venture, creative spark, potential

Reversed: delays, lack of direction, creative block

The first spark of an idea worth chasing, raw potential before it’s been shaped.

Two of Wands

Upright: planning, decisions, looking ahead, personal expansion

Reversed: fear of the unknown, playing it too safe, lack of planning

Standing at the edge of a bigger plan, weighing what’s next before committing.

Three of Wands

Upright: expansion, foresight, looking outward, waiting on progress

Reversed: delays, lack of foresight, unexpected setbacks

Early results starting to show, watching a plan you set in motion begin to unfold.

Four of Wands

Upright: celebration, homecoming, stability, milestone

Reversed: instability at home, delayed celebration, conflict within a community

A real milestone worth marking, a sense of arrival and belonging.

Five of Wands

Upright: conflict, competition, tension, disagreement

Reversed: avoiding conflict, resolving tension, inner conflict

Friction, often the productive kind, competing ideas or people that need to be worked through, not avoided.

Six of Wands

Upright: victory, recognition, public success, confidence

Reversed: private achievement, self-doubt, fall from grace

A win that others can see too, recognition that follows real effort.

Seven of Wands

Upright: defending your position, perseverance, standing your ground

Reversed: giving up, overwhelmed, feeling attacked

Holding your position under pressure, not aggression, but the will to not be pushed off what you’ve built.

Eight of Wands

Upright: fast movement, swift action, alignment, momentum

Reversed: delays, frustration, things slowing down

Things moving quickly now, after a slower buildup, momentum finally released.

Nine of Wands

Upright: resilience, persistence, last stretch of effort, guardedness

Reversed: exhaustion, giving up too soon, paranoia

Tired but still standing, close to the finish with just enough left to see it through.

Ten of Wands

Upright: burden, responsibility, hard work, being overloaded

Reversed: delegating, releasing burden, burnout

Carrying more than one person reasonably should, often self-imposed, worth asking what can be set down.

Page of Wands

Upright: enthusiasm, exploration, a new idea, free spirit

Reversed: lack of direction, impulsiveness, unrealistic plans

Excitement about a new idea before it’s been tested, energy looking for a direction.

Knight of Wands

Upright: action, adventure, impulsiveness, energy

Reversed: recklessness, impatience, scattered energy

Moving fast toward what excites you, sometimes before thinking it fully through.

Queen of Wands

Upright: confidence, determination, warmth, independence

Reversed: jealousy, insecurity, demanding, controlling

Confident warmth that draws people in, independence that doesn’t need permission.

King of Wands

Upright: leadership, vision, boldness, entrepreneurial spirit

Reversed: impulsiveness, high expectations, ruthlessness

Bold vision paired with the will to actually build it, natural leadership.
